What happens on a call

1. She answers

Within a ring or two. She says she's an AI assistant and that the call may be recorded.

2. She listens and asks

The problem, the area, how urgent it is and how to reach the customer. One question at a time.

3. She books

She offers open times from your calendar and books the one the customer picks.

4. You get the details

A short summary of every call, so your team knows who is coming and why.

5. The customer gets a confirmation

By text, when the customer agrees to receive texts.
