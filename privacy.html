Privacy policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

BraniumX LLC ("BraniumX," "we," "us") provides an AI voice receptionist for businesses. This policy explains what we collect and how we use it when you visit braniumx.com, call or text our line, or use our services.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us. When you request a demo, contact us, sign up, or speak with our AI assistant, you may give us your name, phone number, email, company name, industry, the details of your request, and billing information. Payments are handled by our payment processor. We do not store full card numbers.

Information collected automatically. Recordings, transcripts and summaries of calls to our line or to a customer's line; the pages you visit, device and browser type, IP address and approximate location; and cookies and similar technologies.

Information from other sources. Publicly available business information (such as a company's name, website and business phone number) and information from tools that connect to our service, such as calendars.

2. How we use information

  • To provide the service: answering calls, booking appointments and sending confirmations.
  • To process payments and send receipts and account messages.
  • To respond to your questions and give support.
  • To improve, secure and troubleshoot the service, and to prevent fraud and abuse.
  • To send business emails about BraniumX, with an unsubscribe link in each one.
  • To meet legal obligations.

3. Calls and AI

Calls are answered by an AI assistant. At the start of every call it says it is an AI and that the call may be recorded. A caller who does not want to be recorded can say so, and the assistant will end the call and arrange a follow-up by email. Recordings and transcripts are used to deliver the service, to review quality and to fix problems.

4. Text messages

We collect your mobile number when you give it to us, for example on a web form or during a call. We send text messages only when you agree to receive them and only about your request, such as a confirmation, a sign-up link or a follow-up on your call. We do not send promotional text messages from our own line without your separate consent.

  • Opt out: reply STOP at any time. We also honor other clear requests to stop, such as QUIT, END, CANCEL or UNSUBSCRIBE. You may get one last message confirming it.
  • Help: reply HELP or email info@braniumx.com.
  • Frequency: varies with your activity. Message and data rates may apply.
  • No sharing for marketing: we do not sell, rent or share your mobile number or your text-message consent with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes.

5. Email

Business emails from us identify BraniumX, include our postal address and an unsubscribe link, and we honor unsubscribe requests promptly.

6. How we share information

  • Service providers that help us run BraniumX, such as phone, AI, scheduling, email, hosting and payment providers. They may use the information only to provide their service to us.
  • Business transfers, such as a merger or sale of the company.
  • Legal reasons, when the law requires it or to protect rights and safety.
  • With your consent.

We do not sell your personal information.

7. Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies to make the site work, to remember preferences and to understand how the site is used. You can control cookies in your browser settings. Turning them off may affect parts of the site.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information, including secure connections and limited access. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. How long we keep it

We keep information as long as needed to provide the service, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes and enforce our agreements. Then we delete or anonymize it. You can ask us to delete your information at any time.

10. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your information, to object to or limit certain uses, to receive a copy, and to withdraw consent. Email info@braniumx.com and we will respond.

11. Third-party links

Our site may link to other websites. We are not responsible for their privacy practices.

12. Children

BraniumX is for businesses and is not directed to anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you think we have, contact us and we will delete it.

13. International transfers

We are based in the United States. If you use our services from another country, your information will be processed in the United States.

14. Changes to this policy

We will post changes on this page and update the date above.
