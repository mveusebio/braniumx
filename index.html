Questions HVAC owners ask

Will customers know it's an AI?

Yes. Hannah says she's an AI assistant at the start of every call and says the call may be recorded. If someone asks, she says it again. She never pretends to be a person.

Do I have to change my phone number?

No. You keep your number and forward your calls, or you can use a new number.

What if the caller needs a person?

Hannah takes the details and passes them to you. She doesn't handle emergencies. In an emergency she tells the caller to call 911.

Can I cancel?

Yes. It's month to month with no contract, and you can cancel whenever you like.
