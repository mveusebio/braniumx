Built for the way HVAC calls come in.

Breakdowns in July, tune-ups in spring, quotes all year. Hannah answers each one, gets the details, and books it.

Calls Hannah handles

No cooling or no heat

Gets the problem, the address area and how urgent it is, then offers the first open time on your calendar.

Tune-ups and maintenance

Books routine visits so your techs are never stuck waiting for a call back.

Quote requests

Collects the system type, the size of the home and the best way to reach the customer, so your estimator starts prepared.

After-hours calls

Answers nights, weekends and holidays, so the next morning starts with booked jobs, not voicemails.

Common questions

Answers what you tell her to: service area, hours, what you work on. If she doesn't know, she says so and passes it to you.

What every customer hears first.

Honest from the first sentence. Hannah says she is an AI assistant for your company and that the call may be recorded.

If a caller asks whether they are talking to a person, she answers truthfully. If a caller does not want to be recorded, she says someone will follow up and ends the call politely.

Safety comes first

If a caller mentions a gas smell, smoke, fire or a carbon monoxide alarm, Hannah tells them to leave the area and call 911. She does not try to book a job.

What she does not do

  • Diagnose problems or give repair advice
  • Quote final prices unless you give her your price list
  • Take card numbers or payments by phone
  • Replace your dispatcher for emergencies

Works with what you already use

  • Your current phone number: forward your calls, or use a new number
  • Your calendar: Hannah offers only the times you leave open
  • Your team: every call ends with a clear summary of who called and what they need
  • Your rules: your hours, your service area and the jobs you do or don't take
